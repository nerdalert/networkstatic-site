<h2 class="legacy-heading">Help Save A Cat in 2013</h2>
If you are an animal fan like myself there are lots of ways to help save lives. Volunteering at your local shelters, donating money and even something as simple as tweeting links or signing animals advocacy <a href="http://www.thepetitionsite.com/animal-welfare/" target="_blank">petitions</a> can make a difference. The volunteers and operators of Alley Cat Allies at <a href="http://www.alleycat.org" target="_blank">www.alleycat.org</a> do amazing work saving cats everyday.
