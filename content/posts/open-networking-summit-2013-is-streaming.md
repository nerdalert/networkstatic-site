<h2>Open Networking Summit 2013 Is Streaming</h2>

The Open Networking Summit 2013 is going to be live streaming a good chunk of the content. Thats outstanding since most people don't get a chance to travel. The ONS is ground zero for SDN and specifically OpenFlow. It is a great mix of vendors, researchers and <a href="http://packetpushers.net/ons-2013-brings-together-the-entire-sdn-ecosystem-on-april-15-17/" target="_blank">community</a> working out potential future paths of networkings. Head over to see the streams starting Monday April 15th for three days of outstanding presentations including Vincent Cerf from Google.

<h2>Gentle Introduction to Software-Defined Networking</h2>

If you havent looked under the hood of SDN yet, a great primer is by Scott Shenker who is among other things, a PHD faculty member at UC-Berkeley. He views SDN as the proper composition of abstractions that has been the makeup of comp sci and the software development world.
